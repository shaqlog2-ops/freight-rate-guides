## Ocean Freight FCL

FCL (Full Container Load) is the standard mode for this lane. COSCO serves Manzanillo from both Yantian and Shekou terminals in Shenzhen.

| Container Type | Ocean Freight (USD) | Valid Until |
|----------------|---------------------|------------|
| 20GP (20-foot General Purpose) | $5,660 | August 21, 2026 |
| 40GP (40-foot General Purpose) | $5,620 | August 21, 2026 |
| 40HQ (40-foot High Cube) | $5,620 | August 21, 2026 |

**Notes:**
- Rates are port-to-port (SHEKOU/YANTIAN → MANZANILLO)
- COSCO is the primary carrier; other carriers (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM) may offer alternative pricing
- Rates do not include destination charges (THC, documentation, customs clearance)
- Book 2–3 weeks ahead during peak season (June–August) to secure space

## Ocean Freight LCL

LCL (Less than Container Load) is available on this lane for smaller shipments. Contact a freight forwarder for current per-CBM pricing, as LCL rates on this lane fluctuate more frequently than FCL.

## FAQ: Shipping from Shenzhen to Manzanillo

**How much does it cost to ship a 40-foot container from Shenzhen to Manzanillo?**
The current cost is USD 5,620 per 40-foot container (40GP or 40HQ) via COSCO Shipping, valid through August 21, 2026. The 20-foot container (20GP) is slightly more expensive at USD 5,660.

**How long does it take to ship from Shenzhen to Manzanillo by sea?**
Ocean freight transit time is approximately 20–25 days port-to-port (Yantian/Shekou to Manzanillo). Door-to-door delivery typically takes 25–32 days including customs clearance and last-mile delivery.

**Which carriers ship from Shenzhen to Manzanillo?**
COSCO Shipping is the primary carrier on this lane, departing from Yantian and Shekou terminals. Other carriers including Maersk, MSC, and CMA CGM also serve this route with varying schedules and pricing.

**What is the cheapest way to ship from Shenzhen to Mexico?**
For shipments over 500 kg, FCL ocean freight at $5,620 per 40-foot container is the cheapest option. For smaller shipments, LCL (less than container load) is more economical. Air freight costs $6–$10 per kg and is only recommended for urgent, high-value, or very small shipments.

**Do I need to pay customs duties when importing to Mexico?**
Mexico applies MFN (Most Favored Nation) tariff rates on most imports. The specific duty rate depends on the HS code of your goods. Many products benefit from free trade agreements (e.g., Mexico has FTAs with 46+ countries). Consult a customs broker for accurate duty assessment.

**What documents do I need to ship from Shenzhen to Manzanillo?**
Standard shipping documents include: Bill of Lading, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (if claiming FTA benefits), and any product-specific certificates. Mexico also requires a Pedimento (customs declaration) filed by a licensed customs broker.

**Are these freight rates current?**
Yes. The rates in this guide are from COSCO's active rate sheet as of August 16, 2026, and are valid through August 21, 2026. Ocean freight rates change frequently — request a fresh quote before booking.
