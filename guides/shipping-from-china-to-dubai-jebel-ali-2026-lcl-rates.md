## Shipping Cost from Shenzhen to Dubai / Jebel Ali

Freight costs from China to Dubai depend on shipment size and mode. On this lane, LCL consolidation is the most commonly bookable option for small-to-medium shipments. The rate structure is tiered by volume.

<a id="lcl-rates"></a>
## LCL Rate Structure (ex-Shenzhen CFS → Jebel Ali/Dubai)

| Volume Segment | Rate per CBM (USD) |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Under 1 CBM | $34 |
| 3–6 CBM | $39 |
| 6–9 CBM | $49 |
| 9–15 CBM | $54 |

**Notes:**
- Rates are per cubic meter (CBM), ex-Shenzhen CFS (Container Freight Station)
- Minimum charge is 1 CBM
- Carrier: OOCL / CSCL / YML consolidation service
- Destination: Jebel Ali Port, Dubai, UAE
- Rates do not include destination charges (DTHC, documentation, customs clearance)

## FAQ: Shipping from China to Dubai

**How much does it cost to ship from China to Dubai?**
LCL shipping from Shenzhen to Dubai/Jebel Ali costs USD 34 per CBM for shipments under 1 CBM, with tiered pricing up to USD 54 per CBM for 9–15 CBM shipments. Transit time is approximately 12 days. FCL (full container) rates are available on request.

**How long does it take to ship from Shenzhen to Dubai by sea?**
Ocean LCL transit time from Shenzhen to Jebel Ali, Dubai is approximately 12 days port-to-port. Door-to-door delivery typically takes 18–22 days including customs clearance and last-mile delivery.

**What is the cheapest way to ship from China to UAE?**
For shipments under 15 CBM, LCL ocean freight at USD 34 per CBM is the cheapest option. For larger shipments exceeding 15 CBM, FCL (full container load) is typically more cost-effective. Air freight costs $5–$8 per kg and is recommended only for urgent shipments.

**Which port does cargo arrive at when shipping to Dubai?**
Cargo from Shenzhen arrives at Jebel Ali Port (AEJEA), the largest container port in the Middle East and the primary gateway for UAE imports. Jebel Ali is also home to Jebel Ali Free Zone (JAFZA), a major re-export hub.

**Can I ship FCL (full container) from China to Dubai?**
Yes. FCL rates for 20GP, 40GP, and 40HQ containers are available on request. FCL rates on this lane fluctuate weekly — request a fresh quote for current pricing.

**Do I need to pay customs duties when importing to UAE?**
The UAE applies a 5% customs duty on most imported goods (CIF value). Some categories are exempt (e.g., goods for re-export via free zones like JAFZA). Additional charges include VAT (introduced in 2018 at 5%) and potentially excise duty on specific products.
