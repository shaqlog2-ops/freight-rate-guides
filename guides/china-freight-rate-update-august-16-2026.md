## Analysis

**Latin America lanes**: COSCO's active FCL tariff to Latin America remains stable at $5,620–$7,180 per container. Mexico (Manzanillo) and Chile (Valparaiso) share the lowest 40GP rate at $5,620, while Colombia (Cartagena) commands a $1,240 premium at $6,860 — reflecting the longer routing via Panama Canal. Colon Free Zone (Panama) sits in the middle at $6,260 with LCL available at $65/CBM for free-zone re-exporters.

**Middle East lanes**: LCL consolidation from Shenzhen to Dubai/Jebel Ali remains highly competitive at $34/CBM for small shipments. Transit is just 12 days — one of the shortest China-to-Middle East transit times available.

**European lanes**: LCL rates to Northern Europe vary significantly by origin port. Shanghai to Hamburg is $111/CBM via ONE (48-day transit), while Ningbo to Rotterdam is $182/CBM via EMC (34-day transit). The $71/CBM difference reflects both carrier pricing strategies and transit time trade-offs.

**Asia lanes**: Shanghai to Tokyo LCL at $37/CBM with 5-day transit is the shortest and cheapest lane in this update — ideal for small urgent shipments within Asia.
