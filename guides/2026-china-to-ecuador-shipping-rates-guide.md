## About These Rates

### What is Guideline FAK?

Guideline FAK (Freight All Kinds) rates are published tariff rates from COSCO Shipping. They represent the baseline ocean freight charge per container, applicable to general cargo. Actual booking rates may vary based on:

- Cargo type and weight
- Specific shipping line terms
- Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF) and other surcharges
- Peak season surcharges
- Contract vs. spot booking

### Data Source

All rates in this guide are sourced directly from **COSCO Shipping's weekly tariff sheets** and are maintained in the SHAQ Logistics real-time rate database. The database currently holds **443,288 COSCO rate records**, of which **320,776 are active** (valid as of 18 August 2026), updated weekly.

### Important Notes

- Rates do not include destination charges, customs duties, or inland transportation

- All rates are in USD

## FAQ

### What is the current shipping rate from China to Ecuador?

As of August 2026, COSCO Shipping charges $5,660 USD for a 20GP container and $5,620 for 40GP/40HQ from China to both Guayaquil and Posorja.

### How long does shipping take from China to Ecuador?

Container shipping from China to Guayaquil or Posorja takes approximately 22-28 days, with Qingdao/Xiamen departures slightly longer at 24-30 days.

### Which Ecuadorian port is cheaper: Guayaquil or Posorja?

Both Guayaquil and Posorja carry identical COSCO rates of $5,660 (20GP) and $5,620 (40GP/40HQ). Choose based on inland destination and terminal preference.

### What is the difference between 20GP, 40GP, and 40HQ containers?

20GP is a 20-foot standard container (33 cubic meters), 40GP is a 40-foot standard container (67 cubic meters), and 40HQ is a 40-foot high cube container (76 cubic meters, 1 foot taller). 40HQ is preferred for lightweight, bulky cargo.

### Are these rates inclusive of all charges?

No. These are Guideline FAK ocean freight rates only. They do not include destination charges (THC, documentation, customs clearance), inland transportation, customs duties, or surcharges like BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor). Request a full quote for door-to-door pricing.

### How often do shipping rates change?

COSCO Shipping publishes new Guideline FAK rates weekly. Rates can fluctuate significantly based on seasonal demand, fuel prices, and capacity availability. The rate history shown in this guide demonstrates how quickly prices can move during peak season.

### Can I book directly with these rates?
