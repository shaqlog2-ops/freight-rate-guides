## 5. Cheapest Way to Ship a Container to Panama

Given the numbers above, the cost-optimal setup in the Oct 22–31 window is:

1. **Origin: any of the 10 base-rate China ports** — Shanghai, Ningbo, Shekou, Qingdao, Xiamen, Xingang, Dalian, Hong Kong. Flat USD 7,560 / 7,320; no west-coast or east-coast split to optimize.
2. **Equipment: a 40GP.** At USD 7,320 it is USD 240 *cheaper* than a 20GP. Per-CBM math: a 40GP holds roughly 58–67 CBM of practical volume vs ~28 CBM for a 20GP, so even loaded at 75%, the 40GP lands around **USD 110–135 per CBM equivalent** vs **USD 270 per CBM** for a full 20GP — less than half the unit cost.
3. **Gateway: decide by final destination, not freight.** Both PA gateways price identically; pick based on inland trucking to Panama City (via the Bridge of the Americas, ~80 km from Colón) or onward cabotage/transshipment.
4. **LCL caveat:** if you cannot fill even a partial 40GP, compare consolidated LCL quotes. Note that our LCL rate table currently carries no fresh Panama rows (market references commonly quote China → Panama LCL in the **USD 150–250/CBM** range from third-party forwarder publications — verify current levels before committing; below roughly 12–15 CBM, LCL can still lose to a cheap 40GP on this lane given the unusual 40GP pricing).

## 6. Colón Free Zone vs. Manzanillo / Colón Container Terminal

- **Colón Free Zone (PA Door)**: the Western Hemisphere's largest free zone, at the Atlantic entrance of the Panama Canal. Goods admitted for re-export face no Panama import duty — relevant if you are consolidating for distribution into the Caribbean, Central America, or northern South America rather than importing into Panama itself.
- **Manzanillo International Terminal / Colon Container Terminal**: adjacent container facilities optimized for transshipment and direct container handling. Choose this when the box moves onward by vessel or when the Free Zone door-delivery handling is not needed.
- Since COSCO prices both identically (USD 7,560 / 7,320 across all origins), the decision driver is **destination handling cost and final-market paperwork**, not the ocean leg.
- **Atlantic vs. Pacific discharge matters more on Panama than on most routes.** The tariff above covers the Atlantic-side Colón complex (Colón Free Zone / Cristóbal / Manzanillo). If your cargo is bound for **Panama City and the domestic Pacific-side market**, the Pacific-side port of **Balboa** is typically the better discharge — an Atlantic discharge followed by a cross-isthmus move (~80 km overland) adds cost you can avoid by choosing the right side up front. Rule of thumb from third-party Panama routing guides: **free-zone and Caribbean re-export → Atlantic side; Panama City domestic → Pacific side (Balboa).**

### Panama import taxes and paperwork (market context, not part of the freight tariff)

- **Panama has no free trade agreement with China.** Goods leaving the Colón Free Zone into Panama's domestic market pay standard MFN duty (third-party guides put the average around **12%**, most goods in the 3–7% band) plus **7% ITBMS (VAT)**. Goods admitted into the Free Zone for re-export pay **no duty and no ITBMS**.
- **A registered Panamanian customs broker is mandatory** for every commercial shipment entering national customs territory — there is no self-filing option regardless of shipment size.
- **Spot market vs. guideline tariff:** third-party forwarder publications currently quote China → Panama spot FCL roughly **USD 2,000–4,500 per 20GP / 2,500–5,200 per 40ft** (market bands, all-in levels vary by carrier and surcharges). Our USD 7,560 / 7,320 is a carrier **guideline FAK tariff with FAF included** — a bookable published level, typically above transient spot lows. Compare both when booking and request an all-in confirmation.

## 7. FAQ

**Q1: How much does it cost to ship a 40-foot container from China to Panama in October 2026?**
Under COSCO's Caribbean Guideline FAK tariff effective Oct 22–31, 2026: **USD 7,320 per 40GP** from the main China ports, FAF (USD 880) included. The 40GP is USD 240 cheaper than the 20GP on this lane.

**Q2: Why is a 40GP cheaper than a 20GP to Panama?**
That is how COSCO's published tariff is structured this period (USD 7,320 vs USD 7,560). It is the only such inversion in the current Caribbean table. Practical effect: maximize container utilization — per-CBM cost roughly halves on a well-stuffed 40GP.

**Q3: Is Colón Free Zone the cheapest Caribbean destination from China?**
In this tariff, yes — USD 7,560/20GP is the lowest rate across all 17 destinations covered, USD 600 below Kingston/Cartagena/Caucedo and USD 1,100 below Veracruz/Altamira.

**Q4: What is the transit time from Shanghai to Colón?**
The tariff does not publish transit times. Market reference: 30–40 days direct via the Pacific/Panama Canal; Caribbean feeders add 3–7 days. Confirm the sailing schedule at booking.

**Q5: Do rates differ between Shenzhen and Shanghai origins?**
No. Shekou and Shanghai both price at USD 7,560 / 7,320 — the entire Pacific and Southeast China base is flat. Only Fuzhou (+50/+100) and Lianyungang (+100/+100) carry premiums.

**Q6: What is included in the USD 7,560 / 7,320 rate?**
These are FAK guideline rates with the FAF surcharge (USD 440 per 20GP / USD 880 per 40GP) included, per the ACA tariff effective 2026-10-22. Origin THC, documentation, and destination charges at Panama are billed separately — request a full door/port quote.

**Q7: Should I ship to Colón Free Zone or Manzanillo PA?**
The ocean rate is identical. Choose the Free Zone door for duty-deferred re-export distribution into the Americas; choose the container terminals for direct transshipment or Panama customs entry. Factor destination handling rather than freight.

**Q8: What duties apply if I import into Panama's domestic market instead of re-exporting?**
Panama has no FTA with China, so goods leaving the Free Zone for domestic sale pay standard MFN duty (average around 12%, most goods 3–7% per third-party guides) plus 7% ITBMS. A registered Panamanian customs broker is mandatory for every commercial shipment — no self-filing option exists.
