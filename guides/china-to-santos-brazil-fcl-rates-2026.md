# Container Shipping from China to Santos, Brazil: 2026 COSCO FCL Freight Rates & Custo Brasil Cost Guide

**BLUF:** COSCO published FCL rates from 11 major China ports to Santos, Brazil start at **USD 9,950 for a 20GP** and **USD 10,000 for a 40GP/40HQ**, valid through **2026-09-14**. These are origin-agnostic guideline/contract tariff levels across Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen, Qingdao, Tianjin, Dalian, Xiamen and Fuzhou; only Yingkou carries a USD 50–200 premium depending on container size. Forwarder spot-market quotes for non-COSCO or ad-hoc sailings are frequently lower (August–September 2026 ranges: USD 2,500–4,550 for a 20GP and USD 2,500–5,550 for a 40HQ), but the COSCO tariff reflects premium-service contract positioning with predictable space and equipment.

## Custo Brasil: Import Cost Stack

Brazilian landed cost is not just ocean freight. Importers should budget the following mandatory/typical charges on top of the COSCO FCL rate:

| Charge | Typical Rate / Basis | Notes |
|--------|---------------------:|-------|
| Import Duty (II) | 0%–35% of CIF value | HS-code dependent; many industrial inputs at 0–12% |
| IPI (federal excise) | 0%–30% of (CIF + II) | Higher for consumer goods and vehicles |
| PIS / COFINS | 9.25% combined on import value | Federal taxes on imports |
| ICMS (state VAT) | 12%–25% depending on state | São Paulo 18%, Rio de Janeiro 20% |
| AFRMM (merchant marine fund) | ~8% of ocean freight | Applies to most international ocean freight to Brazil |
| SISCOMEX registration | Fixed government fee per declaration | Required for every import declaration |
| RADAR / customs broker | Broker fee + RADAR maintenance | RADAR is the importer registration with Brazilian customs |

The ocean-freight share of a typical landed cost can fall below 10% once import taxes are included, especially for high-duty HS codes.

| Mode | Typical Rate | Best For | Break-Even Rule |
|------|-------------:|----------|-----------------|
| LCL consolidation | USD 80–220 per CBM | 1–15 CBM, multi-supplier cargo | Cheaper than FCL below ~15 CBM |
| 20GP FCL | USD 9,950 (COSCO guideline) | 15–28 CBM | Beats LCL per-CBM cost above ~15 CBM |
| 40HQ FCL | USD 10,000 (COSCO guideline) | 28–68 CBM | Lowest per-CBM cost for volume cargo |

**Caveat:** LCL to Brazil is billed on chargeable volume — the greater of actual CBM or weight-ton equivalent (typically 1 CBM ≈ 1,000 kg). Dense cargo like hardware or auto parts can flip LCL into a weight-based rate that rivals a 20GP earlier than the 15 CBM rule of thumb suggests.

## 2026 Brazil Customs Update: DUIMP

Brazil is replacing the legacy Siscomex DI/LI import declaration with the **DUIMP (Declaração Única de Importação)** under the Single Foreign Trade Portal (Portal Único). Key milestones relevant to maritime imports:

- **January 19, 2026:** Maritime imports with no special legal basis became mandatory DUIMP.
- **March 15, 2026:** DUIMP is in broad enforcement; the system unifies licensing, health-agency approvals (ANVISA/MAPA), and customs clearance in one flow.
- **Four-color risk channel:** Green (automatic release), Yellow (document review), Orange (physical inspection), Red (deep audit/possible seizure).
- **On-water customs clearance (Desembaraço sobre Águas):** Available for maritime imports since March 2026, allowing clearance steps before vessel berthing and potentially reducing storage/demurrage.

For importers, the practical impact is stricter document alignment across commercial invoice, packing list, BL, and DUIMP — especially NCM codes, brand/model, and weights. Any mismatch increases the chance of an orange/red channel assignment and demurrage costs.

### How much does it cost to ship a 40HQ from China to Santos in September 2026?

USD 10,000 for COSCO FCL from Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen (Yantian/Shekou), Qingdao, Tianjin (Xingang), Dalian, Xiamen and Fuzhou, valid through 2026-09-14. Yingkou is USD 10,200 and Lianyungang is USD 10,100.

### Is the 20GP rate to Santos the same from every China port?

No. Nine origins quote USD 9,950, Lianyungang quotes USD 10,000 and Yingkou quotes USD 10,050 for a 20GP. The 40GP/40HQ rate is USD 10,000 from nine origins, USD 10,100 from Lianyungang and USD 10,200 from Yingkou.

### What is the cheapest port to ship from China to Brazil?

For the current COSCO tariff, Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Qingdao, Xingang, Dalian, Xiamen and Fuzhou are tied at USD 9,950 for a 20GP. Choose the port closest to your supplier to minimize domestic trucking cost.

### How long does ocean freight take from China to Santos?

Published COSCO schedules from East Asia to East Coast South America typically range from 35 to 44 days depending on origin and transshipment. North China origins (Qingdao/Tianjin) tend to be at the longer end.

### Are there alternatives to Santos for imports into Brazil?

Yes. COSCO’s same tariff band also serves Itaguai, Itapoa, Navegantes/Itajai, Paranagua/Rio Grande and Rio de Janeiro at the same 20GP/40HQ rate levels. Salvador data in the current tariff is limited to a 20GP rate of USD 10,970.

### What taxes will I pay when importing into Brazil?

Expect import duty (II) of 0%–35%, IPI of 0%–30%, PIS/COFINS of 9.25%, ICMS of 12%–25% and AFRMM of roughly 8% on ocean freight. The total tax burden often exceeds the ocean freight cost.

### Do I need a Brazilian importer registration?

Yes. A RADAR registration with Receita Federal and a SISCOMEX customs declaration are mandatory. Most foreign shippers use a local customs broker or a freight forwarder with RADAR to handle the import.

### When do these rates expire?

### Why are COSCO rates higher than forwarder spot quotes I see online?

The USD 9,950/USD 10,000 figures are COSCO’s published guideline/contract tariff for premium East Coast South America service, valid through 2026-09-14. Forwarder spot-market ranges for non-COSCO or ad-hoc sailings are often lower (e.g., USD 2,500–4,550 for a 20GP) but reflect different carrier/service levels and may exclude BAF, PSS, and destination charges. Use the COSCO tariff for contract budgeting and compare live spot quotes when carrier flexibility is acceptable.

### How long does door-to-door shipping take from China to Brazil?

Typical door-to-door transit is 45–65 days, depending on origin inland trucking, port congestion, and Brazilian customs clearance channel. Port-to-port ocean time is roughly 35–44 days; the remainder is origin trucking, documentation, clearance, and last-mile delivery.

### What port fees should I budget at Santos on top of ocean freight?

Expect destination THC (USD 150–450), storage after free time (USD 8–15/day), demurrage/detention after free time (USD 25–150/day), ISPS (USD 15–25), documentation (USD 50–150), and possible customs exam costs (USD 200–800). Free-time windows and terminal rates vary, so confirm with your forwarder at booking.

### Is LCL cheaper than FCL for small shipments to Brazil?

Yes for cargo below roughly 15 CBM. Market LCL rates to Santos range from USD 80–220 per CBM, with a practical FCL/LCL break-even around 13–18 CBM. Dense cargo may be billed by chargeable weight (1 CBM ≈ 1,000 kg), which can push the break-even lower.

### What changed in Brazilian customs for 2026?

Brazil is migrating from the legacy Siscomex DI/LI to the DUIMP (Declaração Única de Importação) under the Single Foreign Trade Portal. Maritime imports without special regimes became mandatory DUIMP on January 19, 2026. The new system uses green/yellow/orange/red risk channels and stricter document alignment across invoice, packing list, BL, and DUIMP.
