## Cheapest Way to Ship from China to Brazil

For the current COSCO tariff, the cheapest FCL option is any 20GP booking from Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen (Yantian), Shekou, Qingdao, Xingang (Tianjin), Dalian, Xiamen or Fuzhou at **USD 9,950**. A 40GP/40HQ from the same origins is **USD 10,000**, giving a per-TEU cost that is roughly half the 20GP rate—making the larger box the better value for cargo above 20–22 CBM.

When choosing a Brazilian discharge port, consider inland logistics rather than ocean rate alone:
- **Santos** is the default for São Paulo state and the largest southeast industrial corridor.
- **Itapoa / Navegantes** is closer to Santa Catarina’s manufacturing and electronics importers.
- **Paranagua / Rio Grande** serves southern Brazil and Mercosur distribution.
- **Itaguai / Rio de Janeiro** is preferable for Rio de Janeiro state and oil & gas supply bases.

## Custo Brasil: Import Cost Stack

Brazilian landed cost is not just ocean freight. Importers should budget the following mandatory/typical charges on top of the COSCO FCL rate:

| Charge | Typical Rate / Basis | Notes |
|--------|---------------------:|-------|
| Import Duty (II) | 0%–35% of CIF value | HS-code dependent; many industrial inputs at 0–12% |
| IPI (federal excise) | 0%–30% of (CIF + II) | Higher for consumer goods and vehicles |
| PIS / COFINS | 9.25% combined on import value | Federal taxes on imports |
| ICMS (state VAT) | 12%–25% depending on state | São Paulo 18%, Rio de Janeiro 20% |
| AFRMM (merchant marine fund) | ~8% of ocean freight | Applies to most international ocean freight to Brazil |
| SISCOMEX registration | Fixed government fee per declaration | Required for every import declaration |
| RADAR / customs broker | Broker fee + RADAR maintenance | RADAR is the importer registration with Brazilian customs |

The ocean-freight share of a typical landed cost can fall below 10% once import taxes are included, especially for high-duty HS codes.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to ship a 40HQ from China to Santos in September 2026?

USD 10,000 for COSCO FCL from Shanghai, Ningbo, Shenzhen (Yantian/Shekou), Qingdao, Tianjin (Xingang), Dalian, Xiamen and Fuzhou, valid through 2026-09-14. Yingkou is USD 10,200 and Lianyungang is USD 10,100.

### Is the 20GP rate to Santos the same from every China port?

No. Nine origins quote USD 9,950, Lianyungang quotes USD 10,000 and Yingkou quotes USD 10,050 for a 20GP. The 40GP/40HQ rate is USD 10,000 from nine origins, USD 10,100 from Lianyungang and USD 10,200 from Yingkou.

### What is the cheapest port to ship from China to Brazil?

For the current COSCO tariff, Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Qingdao, Xingang, Dalian, Xiamen and Fuzhou are tied at USD 9,950 for a 20GP. Choose the port closest to your supplier to minimize domestic trucking cost.

### How long does ocean freight take from China to Santos?

Published COSCO schedules from East Asia to East Coast South America typically range from 35 to 44 days depending on origin and transshipment. North China origins (Qingdao/Tianjin) tend to be at the longer end.

### Are there alternatives to Santos for imports into Brazil?

Yes. COSCO’s same tariff band also serves Itaguai, Itapoa, Navegantes/Itajai, Paranagua/Rio Grande and Rio de Janeiro at the same 20GP/40HQ rate levels. Salvador data in the current tariff is limited to a 20GP rate of USD 10,970.

### What taxes will I pay when importing into Brazil?

Expect import duty (II) of 0%–35%, IPI of 0%–30%, PIS/COFINS of 9.25%, ICMS of 12%–25% and AFRMM of roughly 8% on ocean freight. The total tax burden often exceeds the ocean freight cost.

### Do I need a Brazilian importer registration?

Yes. A RADAR registration with Receita Federal and a SISCOMEX customs declaration are mandatory. Most foreign shippers use a local customs broker or a freight forwarder with RADAR to handle the import.
