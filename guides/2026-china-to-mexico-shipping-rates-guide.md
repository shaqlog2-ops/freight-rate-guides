## About These Rates

### What is Guideline FAK?

Guideline FAK (Freight All Kinds) rates are published tariff rates from COSCO Shipping. They represent the baseline ocean freight charge per container, applicable to general cargo. Actual booking rates may vary based on:

- Cargo type and weight
- Specific shipping line terms
- Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF) and other surcharges
- Peak season surcharges
- Contract vs. spot booking

### Data Source

All rates in this guide are sourced directly from **COSCO Shipping's weekly tariff sheets** and are maintained in the SHAQ Logistics real-time rate database. The database currently holds **413,720 COSCO rate records**, of which **299,032 are active** (valid as of 18 August 2026), updated weekly.

## FAQ

### What is the current shipping rate from China to Mexico?

As of August 2026, COSCO Shipping rates from China to Mexico's Pacific coast ports (Manzanillo, Lazaro Cardenas, Ensenada) start at $6,000 USD for a 20GP container and $6,300 for a 40GP/40HQ. East coast ports (Altamira, Veracruz) are higher at $8,100 for 20GP and $8,400 for 40GP.

### How long does shipping take from China to Mexico?

Container shipping from China to Mexico takes approximately 20-25 days to Pacific coast ports (Manzanillo, Lazaro Cardenas) and 28-35 days to Gulf coast ports (Altamira, Veracruz) via the Panama Canal.

### Which Mexican port is cheapest for shipping from China?

Manzanillo, Lazaro Cardenas, and Ensenada on Mexico's Pacific coast are the cheapest ports, with identical COSCO rates of $6,000 (20GP) and $6,300 (40GP/40HQ). They are also the fastest to reach from China.

### What is the difference between 20GP, 40GP, and 40HQ containers?

20GP is a 20-foot standard container (33 cubic meters), 40GP is a 40-foot standard container (67 cubic meters), and 40HQ is a 40-foot high cube container (76 cubic meters, 1 foot taller). 40HQ is preferred for lightweight, bulky cargo.

### Are these rates inclusive of all charges?

No. These are Guideline FAK ocean freight rates only. They do not include destination charges (THC, documentation, customs clearance), inland transportation, customs duties, or surcharges like BAF (Bunker Adjustment Factor). Request a full quote for door-to-door pricing.

### How often do China to Mexico shipping rates change?

COSCO Shipping publishes new Guideline FAK rates weekly. Rates can fluctuate significantly based on seasonal demand, fuel prices, and capacity availability. The August 2026 rates shown here represent a 49% increase over July 2026.

### Can I book directly with these rates?

These rates are reference tariff rates. To book a shipment, contact SHAQ Logistics via WhatsApp +86-158-1850-5125 or email ayang@shaq-log.com for a confirmed booking rate that includes all applicable surcharges.
