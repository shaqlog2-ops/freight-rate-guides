# Shipping from China to Chancay Port, Peru: 2026 COSCO FCL Rates — Chancay vs. Callao

> **Data source:** COSCO Shipping "2026 WCSA GUIDELINE TIER" tariff sheet (Sep 22–30, 2026), received directly from COSCO's tariff desk and stored in the SHAQ Logistics rate database
> **Rate type:** Guideline FAK, FCL, FAF included | **Last updated:** September 21, 2026
> **Live rates:** [search.shaq-logistics.com](https://search.shaq-logistics.com)

## Quick Answer

As of the COSCO tariff valid **September 22–30, 2026**, shipping a container from China to **Chancay, Peru** costs **USD 5,500 for a 20GP** and **USD 5,800 for a 40GP or 40HQ** (Guideline FAK from Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Xiamen, Qingdao, Dalian, or Xingang, with the Fuel Adjustment Factor already included). COSCO prices **Chancay exactly the same as Callao** — so the choice between Peru's two Lima-area gateways is an inland-logistics decision, not an ocean-freight decision. COSCO's dedicated Chancay service advertises roughly **23 days Shanghai–Chancay** versus the 28–35 days typical of conventional China–Callao routings.

The origin spread is narrow: at most **USD 120 (20GP)** / **USD 240 (40s)** separates the main Chinese load ports from Japanese feeder origins, so origin-port choice barely moves the ocean freight number.

<a id="chancay-vs-callao"></a>
## 2. Chancay vs. Callao: Which Peru Port?

COSCO's September 22–30 tariff lists **the same FAK level for Chancay and Callao**:

| Destination | 20GP | 40GP | 40HQ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chancay | $5,500 | $5,800 | $5,800 |
| Callao | $5,500 | $5,800 | $5,800 |

That means the decision is operational, not financial:

| Factor | Chancay | Callao |
|---|---|---|
| Operator | COSCO (majority-owned megaport, opened November 2024) | Multi-carrier, Peru's traditional hub |
| Location | ~80 km north of Lima | Lima metropolitan area |
| Ocean rate (COSCO FAK) | $5,500 / $5,800 | $5,500 / $5,800 (identical) |
| Typical direct transit from China | ~23 days (carrier-published schedule) | ~28–35 days on conventional strings |
| Best for | Lima-north cargo, central highlands, new equipment availability, avoiding Callao congestion | Cargo consigned to Callao-terminal customs brokers, Lima-city delivery, multi-carrier options |

**Practical rule:** if your consignee's warehouse or customs broker works Callao, ship Callao; if you want the newer deep-water terminal, faster direct transit, and your cargo is headed to northern/central Peru, Chancay wins at zero ocean-freight penalty on this tariff.

## 4. Other Peruvian Ports: Matarani and Paita

Southern and northern Peruvian gateways sit well above the Lima gateway in current COSCO WCSA tariffs (valid through late September 2026):

| Port | Region | 20GP from | 40GP from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Matarani** | Southern Peru (Arequipa) | $6,500 | $6,800 |
| **Paita** | Northern Peru (Piura) | $7,100 | $7,400 |
| Chancay / Callao (reference) | Lima gateway | $5,500 | $5,800 |

Shipping to Matarani costs roughly **18% more** and Paita roughly **29% more** than the Lima gateway at the 20GP level — driven by lower sailing frequency and feeder coverage. If your final destination is reachable by truck from Lima, routing through Chancay/Callao is normally the cheaper total logistics cost.

|---|---|

The Chancay direct service is the reason the port exists: it compresses Peru transit by roughly **a week to ten days** versus pre-2024 transshipment routings via Mexico or the Caribbean.

<a id="cheapest-way-to-ship"></a>
## 7. Cheapest Way to Ship from China to Peru

- **≥ 1 full 20GP (~28 CBM usable):** book FCL. At USD 5,500 per 20GP, your break-even versus LCL is about **USD 196 per CBM** — if a consolidator quotes LCL above that level, the 20GP is cheaper and faster.
- **< 1 container:** LCL to Callao is quoted per CBM on consolidation services (live quotes via search.shaq-logistics.com). Watch chargeable-weight rules: dense cargo bills on weight/volume whichever yields more revenue.
- **Southern Peru (Arequipa, Moquegua):** compare Chancay + trucking against the direct Matarani call — the ~USD 1,000 ocean premium to Matarani can be wiped out by 1,000+ km of Andean trucking, or justified by it, depending on final city.
- **Northern Peru (Piura, Trujillo):** Paita is closer, but the ~USD 1,600 20GP premium over the Lima gateway usually still favors Chancay/Callao + inland trucking for cost-sensitive cargo.

**Budget note:** Peru imports carry 18% IGV (VAT) on CIF value plus import duty by HS code. Under the **China–Peru FTA**, most goods enter at **0% duty with a valid Certificate of Origin** matched line-by-line to the shipment — without it, SUNAT applies the general 0–11% MFN rate, and the Certificate of Origin cannot be fixed retroactively after clearance. SUNAT clearance typically adds **1–3 working days**.

**Destination-side costs to expect at Callao/Chancay** (categories, quoted case-by-case): destination THC, **Visto Bueno** (the document-release fee charged by the carrier's local agent — a Peru-specific line item that surprises first-time importers), customs examination fees if flagged, and inland drayage. Also note Callao's recurring truck congestion around Avenida Faucett, which can add 24–48 hours to inland delivery into Lima; Chancay's Panamericana Norte drayage is longer in kilometers but currently less congestion-prone.

**Air alternative:** for urgent top-up orders, air freight from major Chinese airports to Lima (LIM) runs roughly 3–8 days on published schedules — worth quoting when ocean transit of 3–5 weeks breaks a launch deadline.

## FAQ

**Q1. How much does it cost to ship a container from China to Chancay, Peru?**
For the COSCO tariff valid September 22–30, 2026: USD 5,500 per 20GP and USD 5,800 per 40GP or 40HQ from Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Xiamen, Qingdao, Dalian, or Xingang, with FAF included. Feeder origins (Korea/Japan) add USD 100–240.

**Q2. Chancay or Callao — which port should I choose?**
On COSCO's current tariff they cost the same ($5,500/$5,800), so choose on operations: Chancay for the ~23-day direct transit, new deep-water equipment, and Lima-north cargo; Callao when your consignee or broker is tied to Callao or you need multi-carrier flexibility.

**Q3. How long does shipping from China to Chancay take?**
COSCO's published direct service runs about 23 days Shanghai–Chancay. Conventional China–Callao strings typically run 28–35 days depending on rotation and transshipment.

**Q4. Are China–Peru container rates rising or falling in September 2026?**
Mixed: the September 22 tariff cut 20GP by 2.8% (USD 5,660 → 5,500) but raised 40GP/40HQ by 3.2% (USD 5,620 → 5,800) versus August 18. The 20GP remains below its August peak; July 24 marked the recent low at USD 4,030.

**Q5. What is the FAF in COSCO's Peru tariff?**
The Fuel Adjustment Factor in this tariff is USD 360 per 20GP and USD 720 per 40GP/40HQ (AAW tariff, effective September 22, 2026) — and it is already included in the rates above, so no need to add it again.

**Q6. Is LCL cheaper than a 20GP to Peru?**
Below roughly 20–25 CBM, yes. The break-even math: USD 5,500 per 20GP ÷ ~28 usable CBM ≈ USD 196 per CBM. If your LCL quote exceeds about USD 196/CBM (or your cargo is dense and bills on chargeable weight), FCL is cheaper.

**Q7. What taxes apply when importing into Peru?**
Peru applies 18% IGV (VAT) on CIF value plus import duty by HS code (commonly 0%, 6%, or 11% under Peru's MFN schedule). Under the China–Peru FTA, most goods enter at 0% duty with a valid Certificate of Origin — without it, the general 0–11% rate applies and cannot be corrected retroactively. SUNAT clearance typically takes 1–3 working days.

**Q8. What are the rates from India to Chancay?**
From the October 2026 COSCO tariff: USD 2,360 per 20GP and USD 2,820 per 40GP from Mundra, Nhava Sheva, Chennai, Colombo, Chittagong, Karachi and other subcontinent ports (USD 2,460/3,020 from Cochin/Tuticorin).
