# Shipping from China to Latin America: 2026 Freight Rate Comparison by Country (Mexico, Panama, Colombia, Chile, Brazil)

**By SHAQ Logistics Rate Desk** | **Updated: August 24, 2026** | **Data source: COSCO carrier rate sheets + WHL/HAM-SUD/EMC LCL tariffs, valid through August 31, 2026**

The cost to ship a 40-foot container from major Chinese ports to Latin America ranges from **USD 7,300 to USD 9,000** depending on destination country, with Mexico (Manzanillo / Lázaro Cárdenas), Chile (Valparaíso / San Antonio), Peru (Callao) and Ecuador (Guayaquil) at the lower end (USD 7,300) and Brazil (Santos / Paranaguá) at the upper end (USD 9,000). Panama (Colón Free Zone) sits at USD 7,500 for a 40GP, while Colombia’s Cartagena is USD 8,100. LCL rates start at USD 7 per CBM to Guayaquil and USD 55 per CBM to Manzanillo. The FCL-versus-LCL break-even point is roughly **13–15 CBM**. All rates below are real, currently-bookable prices from SHAQ’s carrier database valid through August 31, 2026.

## Cheapest Latin America Destination from China

**Answer:** Mexico (Manzanillo / Lázaro Cárdenas), Peru (Callao), Chile (Valparaíso / San Antonio), Colombia (Buenaventura) and Ecuador (Guayaquil) all share the lowest 40GP base ocean freight of **USD 7,300** from major Chinese ports. Mexico wins on transit time (20–25 days) and is the most cost-effective overall for time-sensitive cargo.

**Cost ranking** (40GP, low to high):
1. Mexico / Peru / Chile / Colombia (Buenaventura) / Ecuador: USD 7,300
2. Panama (Colón): USD 7,500
3. Colombia (Cartagena): USD 8,100
4. Brazil (Santos / Paranaguá): USD 9,000

**Why the spread?** Brazil’s higher rate reflects the longest sailing distance, limited direct all-water service and South Atlantic equipment positioning costs. Cartagena’s premium over Buenaventura reflects Caribbean routing demand. Mexico benefits from high volume and direct Pacific services.

## FCL vs LCL: When to Switch

The practical break-even point for China–Latin America shipments is **13–15 CBM**.

- **Under 10 CBM:** LCL is usually cheaper.
- **10–15 CBM:** Quote both; destination CFS and handling fees often tip the balance.
- **Above 15 CBM:** A 20-foot FCL typically becomes cheaper and faster (no consolidation/deconsolidation delays).

A 20ft container holds roughly 28–33 CBM of usable space; a 40ft high cube holds roughly 68–76 CBM.

<a id="customs"></a>
## Country Customs & Tax Notes

### Mexico (2026 tariff update)
- New tariffs on non-FTA countries (including China) effective January 1, 2026.
- 1,463 tariff lines adjusted from 0–20% to 10–50%.
- Peak impact: textiles/apparel, footwear, steel/aluminum, autos and auto parts, plastics, toys, furniture, cosmetics.
- ANAM has tightened HS-code audits; accurate commercial invoice and packing list are critical.

### Colombia
- Customs managed by DIAN through the VUCE single window.
- Import duty (arancel): 0–20% based on HS/NCM code.
- VAT (IVA): 19% on CIF + duty.
- Licensed customs broker (agente de aduanas) required for most commercial imports.
- DIAN cross-references declared values with Chinese export data; under-invoicing risks seizure and fines.

### Brazil
- Only a Brazilian company with CNPJ and RADAR authorization can act as importer of record.
- Import duty (II): 0–35% by NCM.
- IPI: 0–15%+; PIS/COFINS combined ~9.25%; ICMS state VAT 17–18%+.
- AFRMM maritime surcharge: ~8% of ocean freight (current long-distance rate, not the outdated 25%).
- Anti-dumping duties apply to certain Chinese steel, ceramics, textiles and bicycle parts.

## FAQ: Shipping from China to Latin America

**How much does it cost to ship a 40-foot container from China to Mexico?**
A 40GP from Shanghai, Shenzhen, Ningbo or Xiamen to Manzanillo or Lázaro Cárdenas is USD 7,300 via COSCO, valid through August 31, 2026. Transit time is 20–25 days to Manzanillo and 25–30 days to Lázaro Cárdenas.

**How much does it cost to ship from China to Brazil?**
A 40GP from major Chinese ports to Santos or Paranaguá is USD 9,000 via COSCO, valid through August 31, 2026. Transit time is 35–45 days. Total landed cost is dominated by Brazilian import taxes, not ocean freight.

**What is the cheapest Latin American country to ship to from China?**
Mexico, Peru, Chile, Colombia (Pacific coast) and Ecuador all share the lowest 40GP base freight of USD 7,300 from major Chinese ports. Mexico is cheapest when transit time is factored in (20–25 days).

**How long does it take to ship from China to Latin America?**
Transit times range from 20–25 days (China to Manzanillo) to 35–45 days (China to Santos). Panama and Chile fall in the 30–40 day range.

**What is the FCL vs LCL break-even point?**
The break-even is roughly 13–15 CBM. Below 10 CBM, LCL is usually cheaper; above 15 CBM, a 20ft FCL is typically more economical and faster.

**How much does LCL cost from China to Latin America?**
LCL minimums range from USD 7/CBM to Guayaquil (COSCO, 37 days) and USD 32/CBM to Paranaguá (EMC/HMM, 36 days), up to USD 170/CBM to Cartagena (COSCO). Rates are per-CBM and exclude origin/destination handling.

**Does Mexico have new tariffs on Chinese goods in 2026?**
Yes. Effective January 1, 2026, Mexico raised tariffs on 1,463 product lines from non-FTA countries including China, with rates of 10–50% covering textiles, footwear, steel, aluminum, autos and auto parts.

**What taxes apply when importing from China to Colombia?**
Import duty (0–20% by HS code) plus 19% VAT on CIF + duty. DIAN manages clearance through VUCE; a licensed customs broker is required for most commercial shipments.
