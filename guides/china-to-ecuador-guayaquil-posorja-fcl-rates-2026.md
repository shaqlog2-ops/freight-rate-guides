## Guayaquil vs. Posorja: Same Rate, Different Logistics

COSCO's tariff prices **Guayaquil and Posorja identically** across every origin and container type in the 10.22–10.28 file. This mirrors the pattern already observed on the WCSA route (Chile/Peru/Ecuador flat pricing). The decision point is therefore not the ocean freight, but:

- **Posorja** is Ecuador's newer deep-water megaport (Puerto Internacional de Posorja, expanded by DP World), with modern infrastructure and direct access to the Guayas corridor.
- **Guayaquil** is the traditional commercial hub with denser trucking, customs and warehousing ecosystem.
- For cargo destined for Quito or the Sierra, Posorja's highway connection can reduce inland time; for cargo already contracted with Guayaquil-based customs brokers, Guayaquil may stay cheaper on the door-to-door math.

## Cheapest Way to Ship from China to Ecuador

Based purely on the COSCO FAK ocean rate:

1. **Use a 40HQ for cargo >20 CBM.** At USD 5,860, the per-CBM cost is ~USD 146/CBM (assuming 40 CBM usable), versus USD 302/CBM in a 20GP (20 CBM usable) at USD 6,030. **A 40HQ is 52% cheaper per CBM than a 20GP.**
2. **Ship from any main China port.** Flat pricing across Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Qingdao, Xiamen, Dalian and Xingang means the cheapest port is the one closest to your supplier's factory, not the one with the lowest base rate.
3. **Avoid Fuzhou/Lianyungang unless they are your natural loading points.** The +USD 50/100 premium only makes sense if inland trucking to another port costs more than the premium.
4. **Choose Posorja if your final destination is north/west of Guayaquil or if you value modern terminal efficiency; choose Guayaquil for existing broker/warehouse networks.**

## Ecuador Customs & Import Notes

- **Import duty:** Ecuador applies the **Andean Common External Tariff (Arancel Externo Común, AEC)**. Rates vary by HS code; many consumer/industrial goods fall between **0% and 15%**. Verify with your customs broker.
- **VAT (IVA):** Standard rate is **12%** on CIF value + duty + other taxable charges.
- **SENAE / Aduana:** Ecuador's customs authority requires a **RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes)** for the consignee and supports the **VUCE (Ventanilla Única de Comercio Exterior)** for electronic declarations.
- **China–Ecuador FTA:** A **bilateral free-trade agreement between China and Ecuador entered into force on 1 May 2024**, phasing out tariffs on a wide range of products. Exporters should request a **Certificate of Origin (CO)** under the agreement to claim preferential rates.
- **Destination charges:** THC, documentation, customs clearance and inland trucking are not included in the ocean rates above. Budget **USD 400–900** for port handling and customs brokerage at Guayaquil/Posorja, plus inland trucking to Quito or other cities.

## FAQ

**Q1: What is the current FCL rate from Shanghai to Guayaquil?**
A: USD **6,030** for a 20GP and USD **5,860** for a 40GP/40HQ, valid through **28 October 2026** per COSCO's WCSA guideline tariff.

**Q2: Is Posorja cheaper than Guayaquil?**
A: **No.** COSCO prices Posorja and Guayaquil identically — both are USD 6,030 / 20GP and USD 5,860 / 40HQ from main China ports. The choice depends on inland logistics, not the ocean rate.

**Q3: How much did rates increase this week?**
A: Both 20GP and 40GP/40HQ from main China ports increased by **USD 700** week-over-week: 20GP rose **+13.1%** (from USD 5,330 to USD 6,030) and 40s rose **+13.6%** (from USD 5,160 to USD 5,860).

**Q4: Which China origin port is cheapest for Ecuador?**
A: Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Qingdao, Xiamen, Dalian and Xingang are all **flat at USD 6,030 / 20GP and USD 5,860 / 40HQ**. Fuzhou and Lianyungang carry a small premium of +USD 50 on 20GP and +USD 100 on 40s.

**Q5: What is the typical transit time from China to Ecuador?**
A: **28–35 days port-to-port** via the Panama Canal. Direct calls or Posorja-first rotations can be 1–3 days shorter than Guayaquil transhipment.

**Q6: Are LCL rates available in the same COSCO tariff?**
A: **No.** The current COSCO WCSA guideline file does not include LCL rates for Ecuador. LCL quotes must be requested separately.

**Q7: Does the China–Ecuador FTA reduce duty?**
A: Yes, the **China–Ecuador FTA entered into force on 1 May 2024** and provides tariff preferences on many products. A valid Certificate of Origin is required to claim the benefit.

**Q8: Are THC, documentation and customs included in the rates above?**
A: **No.** Published rates are ocean freight only. Destination THC, documentation, customs clearance and inland trucking are additional — typically USD 400–900 at the port plus trucking.
