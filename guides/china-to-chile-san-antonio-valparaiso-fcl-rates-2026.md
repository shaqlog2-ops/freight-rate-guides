## 1. China → Chile FCL Rates (Oct 15–21, 2026)

COSCO published Guideline FAK rates for the South America West Coast (WCSA) trade, valid **October 15 through October 21, 2026**. The Fuel Adjustment Factor (FAF) of **USD 400 per 20GP / USD 800 per 40GP & 40HQ** is **included** in the figures below (AAW tariff basis, effective since 2026-10-15).

| Destination (Chile) | 20GP | 40GP | 40HQ | Currency | Valid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **San Antonio** | 5,330 | 5,160 | 5,160 | USD | 2026-10-15 → 10-21 |
| **Valparaíso** | 5,330 | 5,160 | 5,160 | USD | 2026-10-15 → 10-21 |
| **Iquique** | 5,530 | 5,360 | 5,360 | USD | 2026-10-15 → 10-21 |
| **Arica** | 5,530 | 5,360 | 5,360 | USD | 2026-10-15 → 10-21 |

*Origin basis: all nine main China ports — Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Xiamen, Qingdao, Dalian, Xingang (Tianjin). Carrier: COSCO. Rate type: Guideline FAK, FAF included.*

**Key observations:**

- **San Antonio and Valparaíso are priced identically** — every container type, every origin port. Chile's two central gateway ports are interchangeable on the freight line item; the decision is inland logistics (see Section 7).
- **40GP and 40HQ are priced identically** (USD 5,160). Carriers are not charging a high-cube premium on this trade right now — a free 13% extra volume if your cargo cubes out.
- **Northern Chile costs +USD 200 per box** at every container size (5,530 vs 5,330 on 20GP; 5,360 vs 5,160 on 40s).

## 2. Price Trend: 40GP Down 11% in Two Weeks

Comparing consecutive COSCO WCSA guideline vintages in our database (Shanghai origin, San Antonio/Valparaíso):

| Tariff vintage | 20GP | 40GP / 40HQ | Change (40s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jul 24, 2026 (summer low) | 4,030 | 4,030 | baseline |
| Aug 18, 2026 | 5,660 | 5,620 | +39.5% |
| Sep 22 – Oct 14, 2026 | 5,500 | 5,800 | +3.2% |
| **Oct 15–21, 2026** | **5,330** | **5,160** | **−11.0%** |

Three concrete facts exporters can quote right now:

1. **40GP/40HQ dropped USD 640 per box (−11.0%)** between the Oct 1–14 vintage and the Oct 15–21 vintage — the first decline on this trade since the July low.
2. **20GP dropped USD 170 (−3.1%)** (5,500 → 5,330) in the same window.
3. The **FAF component rose 11.1%** (USD 360 → 400 per 20GP; USD 720 → 800 per 40s) — meaning the underlying base rate fell even faster than the headline: the base 40s rate dropped roughly **USD 720 (−12.4%)** while fuel surcharge rose only USD 80.

Direction of travel: after peaking at 5,800 (40s) in early October, second-half October pricing has reset to 5,160. Spot-adjacent FAK pricing is softening into late October.

COSCO applies a **flat WCSA pricing structure**: every mainland port from Guayaquil south to San Antonio costs the same. This is our third consecutive vintage observing this pattern, and it keeps confirming the same conclusion: **the carrier does not price the port choice — your inland leg does.** For cargo bound for Santiago, freight to San Antonio = freight to Callao; total landed cost is decided by Chilean trucking vs Peruvian trucking, duty treatment, and destination charges.

<a name="cheapest-way"></a>
## 7. Cheapest Way to Ship a Container to Chile

Given the Oct 15–21 tariff, the cost-minimizing playbook is:

1. **Ship 40HQ, not 2× 20GP.** A 40HQ costs USD 5,160 vs USD 10,660 for two 20GPs — the 40HQ moves **~2.3× the volume (76 vs 33 CBM practical)** and works out to **58% less per cubic meter** (USD 68/CBM vs USD 162/CBM at the 40HQ vs 20GP headline). Only pay for 20GP if weight, not volume, caps you out (~28 t payload).
2. **Load at any of the nine main ports.** There is zero freight-rate penalty for origin choice among Shanghai/ Ningbo/ Yantian/ Shekou/ Hong Kong/ Xiamen/ Qingdao/ Dalian/ Xingang — pick the one with the shortest (and cheapest) inland leg.
3. **Central Chile → San Antonio; Valparaíso only for specific consignee terminals.** Identical ocean freight; San Antonio handles the larger share of Chile's container volume and often has better trucking availability to Santiago (~100–120 km for both).
4. **Northern Chile / Zofri-bound cargo:** accept the USD 200 premium to Iquique/Arica, or price the alternative of discharging San Antonio and trucking north (~1,300+ km — almost never wins).
5. **Time the vintage, not the market.** COSCO reprices in 7-day tiers (this one: Oct 15–21). A booking that crosses a vintage boundary can move ±11%, as the 40s just did. Confirm the tariff week before you lock a sales contract with a fixed-freight clause.
6. **FAK is not your floor.** This table is the carrier's published guideline (essentially the ceiling most NVOCCs mark up). Shippers with 10+ containers per month typically contract 15–25% below guideline FAK — worth testing before you accept the published number.

<a name="customs"></a>
## 8. Chile Customs: FTA, Duty & IVA

*Public trade-policy knowledge, provided for orientation — not customs advice.*

- **Chile–China FTA** (in force since July 2006): the majority of tariff lines carry **0% duty** with a valid certificate of origin (issued in China by CCPIT or authorized bodies). This makes Chile one of the few Latin American markets where import duty is generally *not* a cost line for Chinese-origin goods — unlike Colombia (no FTA) or Mexico (most sectors covered, but rules of origin are stricter).
- **IVA (VAT) is 19%**, applied on CIF value + duties. Unlike Peru (where IVA is generally recoverable), Chilean IVA applies to imports and is creditable against output VAT for registered importers — plan cash flow accordingly.
- **Customs authority:** Servicio Nacional de Aduanas de Chile. Standard import requires a Declaración de Ingreso; most containers clear within 1–3 business days when documents (B/L, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin) are pre-filed.
- **San Antonio and Valparaíso both sit ~100–120 km from Santiago** via Routes 78 and 68 respectively; inland trucking to the capital is the single largest variable destination cost after IVA cash flow.

## 9. FAQ

**Q1. How much does it cost to ship a 40ft container from China to Chile in October 2026?**
A 40GP or 40HQ costs **USD 5,160** (FAF included) from any of the nine main Chinese ports to San Antonio or Valparaíso on COSCO's published tariff valid October 15–21, 2026. That is USD 640 (−11.0%) lower than the first-half-October tariff of USD 5,800.

**Q2. What is the 20GP rate from Shanghai to San Antonio?**
**USD 5,330** including the USD 400 FAF, valid 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-21. Shanghai, Ningbo, Yantian, Shekou, Hong Kong, Xiamen, Qingdao, Dalian and Xingang all share the same rate.

**Q3. Which Chilean port is cheaper — San Antonio, Valparaíso, or Iquique?**
San Antonio and Valparaíso are identical (5,330/5,160). Iquique and Arica cost USD 200 more per box (5,530/5,360). For Santiago-central cargo there is no price reason to prefer either central port.

**Q4. Is it cheaper to ship to Chile or Peru?**
On COSCO's current tariff they are the same: Callao/Chancay and San Antonio/Valparaíso all price at USD 5,330 (20GP) / USD 5,160 (40s). Total landed cost differences come from inland transport and duty treatment, not the ocean leg.

**Q5. Why did 40ft rates to Chile drop 11% in October 2026?**
The Oct 15–21 guideline reset 40GP/40HQ from USD 5,800 to USD 5,160. Note the FAF actually rose from USD 720 to 800 — so the base rate fell roughly 12.4%. We do not speculate on carrier pricing motives, but the direction across the last two vintages is clearly downward into late October.

**Q6. How long does shipping from China to Chile take?**
Typical carrier-published schedules run ~32–40 days port-to-port for direct WCSA loops (San Antonio/Valparaíso), and ~40–50 days via transshipment. Confirm the specific rotation at booking.

**Q7. Do I pay import duty on Chinese goods into Chile?**
Generally no, if your goods qualify under the China–Chile FTA and you present a certificate of origin — most tariff lines are at 0%. Chilean IVA of 19% on CIF still applies and is creditable for registered importers.

**Q8. Is there an LCL rate table for Chile?**
Our current carrier feed contains no fresh LCL per-CBM rows for the Chile trade (FCL guideline data only). For cargo under ~15 CBM, request a consolidated quote — at the current 40HQ level (USD 5,160), LCL only wins below roughly 8–10 CBM depending on destination charges.
